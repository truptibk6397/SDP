About Karnataka

Karnataka is a beautiful state located in the southwestern region of India. Bengaluru is the capital city of Karnataka.

Karnataka is famous for its rich history, magnificent monuments, beautiful landscapes, wildlife, traditional culture and delicious food.

From the ancient ruins of Hampi to the green hills of Coorg and the spectacular Jog Falls, Karnataka offers many experiences for travellers.

Did You Know?

Karnataka has a diverse landscape that includes beaches, forests, waterfalls, hills and historical monuments.

Famous Tourist Places

Mysuru

Mysuru is famous for Mysore Palace, cultural traditions and Dasara celebrations.

Hampi

Hampi is known for its ancient temples, monuments and fascinating ruins.

Coorg

Coorg is known for its coffee plantations, forests and beautiful mountain scenery.

Gokarna

Gokarna is a coastal destination famous for its beaches and temples.

Historical Places

Hampi

Hampi was an important centre of the Vijayanagara Empire and contains numerous historic monuments.

Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is one of the most famous landmarks of Mysuru and is known for its grand architecture.

Gol Gumbaz

Gol Gumbaz in Vijayapura is a remarkable monument known for its large dome and architectural design.

Badami Caves

The Badami cave temples are famous for their rock-cut architecture and sculptures.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Jog Falls

One of the spectacular waterfalls of Karnataka, located in the Western Ghats region.

Chikmagalur

A scenic hill region famous for coffee estates, forests and mountain views.

Kemmanagundi

A beautiful hill station surrounded by greenery and mountain landscapes.

Famous Foods of Karnataka

Masala Dosa

A popular South Indian dish made with a crispy fermented rice and lentil crepe.

Bisi Bele Bath

A traditional Karnataka dish prepared using rice, lentils, vegetables and spices.

Ragi Mudde

A traditional dish made from ragi flour and commonly served with curry.

Mysore Pak

A popular sweet prepared using gram flour, ghee and sugar.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit

Karnataka can be visited throughout the year. The ideal season depends on the destination and activities planned.

Transportation

Karnataka has airports, railway networks, highways and bus services connecting major cities and tourist destinations.

Accommodation

Travellers can find hotels, resorts, homestays and other accommodation options in major tourist destinations.

Travel Tips

Carry comfortable clothing, keep important documents safe and check local conditions before travelling.
